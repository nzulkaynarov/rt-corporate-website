## 4. Landed Cost DDP Tashkent Calculation Formula

When delivering under DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) Tashkent to local enterprises:
* **Base Invoice (FCA / FOB):** Foreign factory price in USD or RUB.
* **Railway Freight & Insurance:** Typically 6% - 12% depending on route.
* **Customs Duty (Пошлина):** 0% - 15% according to HS Code (ТН ВЭД).
* **Uzbekistan VAT (НДС):** Strictly **12%** calculated on (Customs Value + Duty). Fully deductible by the buyer in Didox.
* **Fixed Currency Contract:** Prices are locked in Uzbek Sums (UZS), shielding buyers from exchange rate risks.
