## Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find the answer? Contact us, and we'll respond within one business day.

{{< accordion separated=true >}}

{{< accordionItem title="How do I request a commercial quote?" >}}
Register in the Partner Portal, complete a quick company verification (KYC), browse the catalog to select the items you need, and click 'Request Quote.' A dedicated manager will prepare your proposal and deliver it through the portal.
{{< /accordionItem >}}

{{< accordionItem title="Do you work with companies outside Uzbekistan?" >}}
Yes, we partner with companies from Kazakhstan, Russia, and other CIS countries. For non-resident clients, we offer DAP and CIF delivery terms with full documentation support.
{{< /accordionItem >}}

{{< accordionItem title="What are the typical delivery timelines?" >}}
Timelines depend on stock availability and country of origin. In-stock items ship within 3 to 10 business days. Made-to-order items are quoted individually when your proposal is prepared.
{{< /accordionItem >}}

{{< accordionItem title="Do you provide certificates for the equipment?" >}}
Yes, all products are accompanied by conformity certificates, quality passports, and manufacturer declarations. Documents are accessible in the Partner Portal after verification.
{{< /accordionItem >}}

{{< accordionItem title="Is it possible to return a product?" >}}
Returns are accepted within 14 days, provided proper storage conditions are maintained and packaging is intact. Terms are specified in the supply agreement generated through the Partner Portal.
{{< /accordionItem >}}
