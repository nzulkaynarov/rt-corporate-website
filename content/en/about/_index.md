{{< lead >}}
An innovation-driven technology company founded in 2021 in Tashkent to provide professional services in equipment supply and engineering project implementation.
{{< /lead >}}

**LLC RailTech** was established by experienced industry professionals to deliver expert services in equipment supply, engineering design, and technical project implementation. We are an authorized dealer and official representative of leading international manufacturers of industrial and railway equipment in Uzbekistan.
